The Phoenician Scheme

The Phoenician Scheme is a 2025 black comedy espionage thriller film written, produced, and directed by Wes Anderson from a story he conceived with Roman Coppola.

Time to know a bit about Wes Anderson

Wesley Wales Anderson is an American filmmaker. His films are known for themes of grief, loss of innocence, and dysfunctional families.
Wes Anderson is celebrated for his unmistakable visual and narrative style, defined by meticulous symmetry, vibrant pastel color palettes, and eccentric, deadpan characters. As a true cinematic auteur, he maintains complete creative control over his projects by writing, directing, and producing them.
Every Wes Anderson film reckons a good amount of unique characteristics.

Wes Anderson unique way to make a movie

  • Symmetry & Centered Framing: He famously positions his subjects dead center in the frame, creating beautifully balanced, geometric compositions
  • Distinct Color Palettes: Every film uses a highly specific color scheme to evoke a specific mood
  • Precise Camera Work: Anderson favors flat, two-dimensional camera movements,
    utilizing quick 90-degree whip pans and perfectly parallel tracking shots that feel like a storybook coming to life.

Summary of "The Phoenician Scheme"

The story follows Anatole "Zsa-Zsa" Korda, a wealthy and ruthless industrialist, arms dealer, and European oligarch. After surviving multiple assassination attempts and a plane crash—which causes him to have visions of the afterlife featuring Willem Dafoe—Korda decides to secure his legacy. He recruits his estranged daughter, Liesl, who is currently in the middle of her novitiate to become a Catholic nun. Having disinherited his eight opportunistic sons, Korda names Liesl his sole heir, convincing her to take on the role for a probationary period.
The central plot—the titular "Phoenician Scheme"—is an ambitious and complex infrastructure project designed to industrialize the Phoenician region and expand his fortune. However, a massive financial deficit forces Korda and Liesl onto a global tour. Accompanied by their assistant and tutor, Bjørn, they travel the world to negotiate with rival investors and frenemies (played by Tom Hanks, Bryan Cranston, Scarlett Johansson, Benedict Cumberbatch, and Jeffrey Wright) while evading mercenaries, terrorists, and secret agents.
As the story unfolds, it is revealed that Bjørn is actually a secret agent named Carson. In the climax, Korda uses his fortune to fairly pay his workers, defeats his half-brother and would-be assassin Nubar, undergoes a spiritual conversion, and retires to open a small bistro with Liesl, who ultimately accepts Bjørn's marriage proposal.

The shoeboxes

In the film, Anatole "zsa-zsa" Korda uses shoeboxes to organize his plans, documents, goods and the various stages of his ambitious industrial and infrastructure empire.
They are also used to divide the story into chapters.

Prologue: The Plane Crash & The Appointment of Liesl Chapter I/Shoebox nº1: The Prince of Phoenicia (Prince Farouk / Riz Ahmed) Chapter II/Shoeboxnº2 : The Sacramento Moguls (Leland & Reagan / Tom Hanks & Bryan Cranston)
Chapter III/Shoebox nº3: The Newark Shipping Tycoon (Marty / Jeffrey Wright) Chapter IV/Shoebox nº4: The Nightclub & Foreign Syndicate (Marseille Bob / Mathieu Amalric) Chapter V/Shoebox nº5: The Cousin & Utopian Vision (Cousin Hilda / Scarlett Johansson)
Epilogue: Reckoning, Bankruptcy & The Bistro (Uncle Nubar / Benedict Cumberbatch)
